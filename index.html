Meet the Founder

Built on a simple idea.

Flaunt Theory started in Mumbai with one belief: style should feel easy, comfortable, and still make you feel confident. Not a costume you put on for the occasion — clothes you'd actually want to be photographed in.

Every piece is designed in-house and produced in small batches, so what you're wearing isn't in every third feed you scroll past.
